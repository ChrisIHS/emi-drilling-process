A Reclamation Certificate is the milestone that removes a site from the operator's inactive liability inventory and retires the associated ARO from the balance sheet. Reclamation and remediation spend qualifies toward the AER mandatory closure spend quota alongside downhole abandonment. Sites awaiting certification continue to accrue surface lease rentals, municipal taxes and inspection costs — the carrying-cost burn shown above.
